title: NZNOG 2027 Dinner
---
**6.00pm - 10.00pm** <br />
**Te Puia, Rototua**<br />

The Thursday night conference dinner will be held at Te Puia. We look forward to seeing you there!

Buses will depart Millenium Hotel at 5.45pm.

On arrival we will be welcomed by Pōwhiri at Te Heketanga (the carved pillar entrance), followed by drinks and a buffet dinner at Te Whakaruruhau Grand Hall along with a Marama light and laser show at the geysers.

Buses will return guests to Millenium Hotel after dinner.

The dinner is open to partners. Please register them as an additional attendee, and tick just the "partner ticket" option so we can ensure everyone is seated and catered for.
