title: NZNOG 2027 Dinner
---
**6.00pm - 10.00pm** <br />
**Te Puia, Rotorua**<br />

The Thursday night conference dinner will be held at Te Puia. We look forward to seeing you there!

Buses will depart Millennium Hotel at 5.45pm.

On arrival we will be welcomed by Pōwhiri at Te Heketanga (the carved pillar entrance), followed by drinks and a buffet dinner at Te Whakaruruhau Grand Hall along with a Marama light and laser show at the geysers.

Buses will return guests to Millennium Hotel after dinner.

If your partner is travelling with you they are welcome to come to dinner. Please email [info@nznog.org](mailto:info@nznog.org?subject=NZNOG 2027 Partner Dinner Ticket") for a code. Note that there is a cost of $50.
